Featured Project

Communication-Aware Distributed MPC for DC Microgrids

A new project concept at the intersection of distributed control, co-simulation, and smart-grid operation.

All Distributed Control Co-Simulation Smart Grids

Project Summary

This project investigates a communication-aware distributed model predictive control (DMPC) framework for DC microgrids, where local controllers coordinate power-sharing and voltage regulation while explicitly accounting for communication constraints such as latency, packet loss, bandwidth limits, and asynchronous information exchange.

The goal is to combine distributed optimization, resilient control design, and high-fidelity co-simulation to evaluate how communication quality affects closed-loop performance, stability margins, and scalability in networked energy systems.
